Understanding Semantic HTML

Published on March 15, 2025

What is Semantic HTML?

Semantic HTML means using HTML elements for their intended purpose, improving accessibility and SEO.

Some of the semantic tags are:

  • header
  • main
  • section
  • article
  • footer
  • figure
  • figcaption
  • form
  • details
  • mark
  • nav
  • summary
  • time

Article Tag:

An article is a Self-Contained Content Block.Represents independent, self-contained content that can stand alone. Used for blog posts, news articles, forum posts, or user comments. Can be shared or syndicated independently. Use article tag when the content is self-contained and makes sense even if taken out of context.
